The Umbrella Man | Stop-Motion Game Concept Trailer

A small story about loss, memory, and the things we carry with us.

Umbrella Man is a narrative-driven game concept presented through a handcrafted stop-motion aesthetic. This trailer explores the atmosphere, visual language, and emotional tone of the game.

Genre: Narrative / Adventure
Format: Game Concept
Art Style: Stop Motion / Handmade
Tone: Melancholic • Emotional • Mysterious
Timeline: 2 Weeks (Including Pre and Post production)
Team Members: Sagar Sigroha, Thavanesh N, Aashish D & Bhaswati G
